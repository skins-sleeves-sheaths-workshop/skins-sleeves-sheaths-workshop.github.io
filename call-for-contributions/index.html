Call for Contributions

We invite extended abstracts on scalable robotic materials that sense, actuate and conform, from new materials and fabrication methods to perception, control and applications.

Scope

We welcome contributions covering, but not limited to:

  • Scalable materials and fabrication for large-area functional coverings
  • Sensing skins: multimodal tactile, thermal and proximity sensing, readout and calibration
  • Actuating and morphing layers: soft actuators, variable stiffness and shape change
  • Conformable integration: mounting on curved and moving bodies, robustness and repair
  • Perception and learning from distributed material signals, including robot foundation models
  • Applications: manipulation, whole-body contact, human-robot interaction, wearables and assistive devices

Submission instructions

Extended abstracts may contain previously published material and therefore do not require anonymization. Please list all authors and affiliations, and cite any prior related work by the same authors.

Submissions can present preliminary results or summarize already published work. In the latter case, state this clearly, e.g. "This work is an extract from the paper [x]".

  • Length: up to 2 pages including figures and tables, plus 1 additional page for references
  • Format: IEEE two-column, US letter (templates)
  • Anonymization: not required

Submission portal: opening soon

Review and presentation

All submissions are peer reviewed by the organizers and invited reviewers, based on relevance to the workshop, technical quality and originality, clarity, and potential to stimulate discussion. Extended abstracts will not be published in formal proceedings.

Accepted contributions will be presented as posters with a short flash talk. At least one author must present in person in Seoul. Interactive demonstrations are very welcome.

  • Poster size: A0 portrait (recommended)
