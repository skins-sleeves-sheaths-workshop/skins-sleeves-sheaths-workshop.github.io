About the Workshop

Materials that cover, move and feel

Robots are moving out of the lab and into homes, hospitals and fields, where they touch people, objects and surfaces with almost every part of their body. Yet most robots are still built as rigid machines with sensing and compliance added at a few points, usually the fingertips.

This workshop asks what happens when the material covering a robot does the work: skins that sense contact across the whole body, sleeves that actuate and reshape the limb they wrap, and sheaths that conform to complex geometry while protecting what lies underneath. We bring together researchers in soft materials, flexible electronics, fabrication and robot learning to discuss how such materials can be made at scale, integrated onto real robots, and turned into useful signals for perception and control, including as inputs to learning-based and foundation-model policies.

Sense

Skins

Large-area, multimodal tactile and proprioceptive skins, from printed electronics to impedance-based and optical sensing.

Actuate

Sleeves

Wearable and wrap-around actuating layers that stiffen, morph or drive the bodies they cover, for robots and for people.

Conform

Sheaths

Compliant, protective coverings that conform to complex geometries and survive real-world contact, wear and repair.

Topics of Interest

What we will discuss

  1. 01
    Scalable materials and fabricationPrinting, knitting, casting, molding and roll-to-roll approaches for large-area functional coverings.
  2. 02
    Sensing skinsMultimodal tactile, thermal and proximity sensing; wiring, readout and calibration at scale.
  3. 03
    Actuating and morphing layersSoft actuators, variable stiffness and shape-changing materials integrated into coverings.
  4. 04
    Conformable integrationMounting on curved and moving bodies, robustness, self-healing, repair and replaceability.
  5. 05
    Perception and learning from material signalsRepresentations for distributed sensing and their use in control and robot foundation models.
  6. 06
    ApplicationsManipulation, whole-body contact, safe human-robot interaction, wearables and assistive devices.
